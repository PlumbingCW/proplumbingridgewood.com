Whole House Repiping in Ridgewood, NJ.

Repiping replaces the supply lines that carry water through your walls, floors and ceilings. It is a big decision, and it should be made from evidence, not a sales pitch.

Signs the supply piping is nearing the end.

Old pipes tell on themselves. Brownish water from the tap, low pressure at the far end of the house, and pinhole leaks that keep reappearing in different places all point to wear across the system rather than a single bad section.

Galvanized steel corrodes from the inside and narrows over time. Copper can develop pinholes. Some plastic systems from certain eras have a record of failing at fittings. When leaks keep multiplying, tracing each one with leak detection and patching it stops being the economical choice.

Repipe everything, or repair one section?.

A repair or partial repipe fits when.

  • Only one run or one floor is failing.
  • The rest of the pipe tests sound.
  • The house is recently renovated with new lines in places.
  • You need a stopgap before a larger project.

A whole house repipe fits when.

  • Leaks keep appearing in different spots.
  • Pressure is poor because pipes are internally narrowed.
  • The pipe material is known to fail.
  • You are already opening walls for a remodel.

If a line has already burst, see our burst pipe repair page. This page is for planned work, where you decide the timing.

Lead and galvanized lines: the service line piece.

Repiping inside the house is separate from the service line that runs from the main to your building. New Jersey law requires lead and galvanized service lines to be found and removed by 2031.

Ridgewood Water, which also serves Glen Rock, Midland Park and Wyckoff, has identified more than 3,000 lines that are wholly or partly lead. The public side, from the main to the curb, is owned by the utility. The private side, from the curb to the building, belongs to the homeowner.

If you hire your own contractor for the private side, you need to coordinate with Ridgewood Water. The utility's customer line is 201-670-5520. Our water service line repair page covers that work, and it often makes sense to plan it alongside interior repiping.

Ridgewood Water's 2019 data report put lead at the 90th percentile at 1.92 ppb, under the 15 ppb action level, with none of 31 samples over. That reflects tap sampling, not your pipes specifically. If you have galvanized or old lead-bearing parts, replacing them is still the cleaner path.
