Read before the problem, or during it.

Most plumbing trouble gives some warning. A drain that gurgles, a water heater that rumbles, or a bill that creeps up each month are all signals that something is changing inside your pipes. The guides below explain what those signs usually mean and what a homeowner can safely check.

We write each article around a real question, such as why a basement floods after rain or what to do first when a pipe splits. The goal is to give you clear steps, plain language and an honest view of when a job is worth a professional visit.

None of these guides replace a call when water is actively running or sewage is backing up. If that is happening now, close the nearest shutoff valve and call (973) 488-5410. A dispatcher will answer at any hour and walk you through the next steps.

If you are planning ahead, take your time. Read the article that fits your situation, note the questions it raises, and bring them to the plumber when you schedule a visit.
