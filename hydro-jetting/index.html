What hydro jetting does differently.

A jetter feeds a flexible hose with a nozzle into the pipe. Water leaves through angled jets that push the nozzle forward and spray backward, stripping buildup from the pipe wall as it returns.

The result is a line scrubbed close to its full diameter, not a narrow channel through the middle. That matters for pipes that clog again within weeks after a cable pass.

Jetting or cabling.

Hydro jetting suits.

  • Grease and soap scum coating the pipe.
  • Mineral scale and sludge buildup.
  • Root fibers left after a cutter pass.
  • Annual maintenance on a problem main line.

Cabling suits.

  • A single hard stoppage such as a toy or a mass of paper.
  • Sink, tub and shower branch clogs.
  • Lines too fragile or too small for jetting.
  • Quick relief when the line is fully blocked.

Often the answer is both. A cable can open a completely blocked line so that the jetter can clean it. Our drain cleaning service covers cabling and the camera work in between.

Signs a line is ready for jetting.

  • The same drain clogs every few weeks despite repeated cabling.
  • Slow drains in several fixtures with no single blockage found.
  • Gurgling and a sewer gas smell with no visible backup.
  • Grease from a kitchen line or a restaurant floor drain.
  • Roots cut earlier that are now growing back.

When jetting is the wrong tool.

We scope the pipe first.

High-pressure water can worsen a cracked, offset or collapsed pipe, and it can push loosened debris into a belly. That is why we run a camera before we jet an unknown line, particularly an old one.

If the camera finds a break, the answer is repair, not more pressure. Go straight to sewer line repair and clean the line afterward.

Emergency steps if the line has backed up.

If wastewater is coming up through a floor drain, tub or toilet, do this while you wait.

Stop using water.

No flushing, laundry, dishwasher or showers. Water you add has nowhere to go but out of the lowest drain.

Keep the cleanout reachable.

Clear boxes and stored items from around the cleanout so access is quick. Leave the cap on if sewage is rising.

Do not add chemicals.

Drain openers leave caustic liquid in the pipe and can injure whoever works on it.

Guard the floor.

Lift valuables, put down towels and keep children and pets clear until the area is cleaned.

Ridgewood pipes and the jetting decision.

According to the Village sewer ordinance in Chapter 238, house sewers must be cast iron or vitrified clay beyond three feet from the building, and parts of Ridgewood date from the 1890s through about 1950. Old pipes can carry heavy scale and roots, which suits jetting, but they can also be fragile at the joints.

Ridgewood Water's published reports show hardness from about 136 to 470 parts per million. Hard water leaves mineral scale on pipe walls over the years, and jetting is one of the few ways to remove it without scraping the pipe.

A check on the pipe and a cleaning plan fit together. If the camera shows a damaged lateral, we explain the fix, and if the line is sound, we jet it and tell you how often to repeat it. A restaurant or shop on Ridgewood Avenue, or a multi-unit building, may need a regular schedule.
