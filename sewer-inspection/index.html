What a sewer camera inspection actually shows.

The camera travels from a cleanout or an accessible fixture drain toward the street connection. As it goes, it shows the pipe material, joints, cracks, sags, roots, scale and any foreign object. A locator tool at the surface lets us mark the pipe's depth and path, which matters if you plan to dig.

You leave with an understanding of what the line looks like and what, if anything, needs doing about it. A pipe in decent shape may need nothing. A pipe with a break or a belly needs a plan.

Good reasons to schedule one.

  • You are buying a home and want to know what the lateral is hiding before closing..
  • You plan to dig for a patio, pool, fence, addition or large tree and need the pipe's path and depth..
  • Drains in the house keep slowing, and snaking has not changed anything for long..
  • You are considering lining or replacing the pipe and need a baseline..
  • You want to confirm a repair worked..

If the picture is bad, the next step may be sewer line repair by spot dig or lining. If the pipe is only dirty, drain cleaning or hydro jetting may be enough, and the camera shows which.

What Ridgewood's sewer rules mean for your lateral.

According to Chapter 238 of the Village of Ridgewood Code, house sewers must be cast iron or vitrified clay beyond three feet from the building, and that they must sit deep enough to be protected from frost. So you may find either cast iron or clay in a Ridgewood lateral, and each shows its own typical wear on camera. Cast iron scales and corrodes, and clay tends to open at joints.

The same code prohibits connecting groundwater sumps to the sanitary sewer. If the camera shows an unexpected tie-in or a lot of clear water entering the pipe, that finding matters. We explain what it means for compliance before you decide what to do.

Ridgewood runs its own Water Pollution Control Facility, so the pipe in the street leads to a village-operated plant. Your lateral is the stretch between your house and that public main, and a recorded inspection gives you a clear picture of it.
